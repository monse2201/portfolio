Vitta Health — Founder & AI Product Lead
AI clinical documentation platform
173 commits, solo 20 physicians 415 consultations
View case ↗

Vitta Health (product name VittaScribe) is the current, focused version of an idea I started as CareConnect and later grew into Movy Health — the name you'll see in some of the press and TV coverage below. Same founder, same journey, evolved product.

Problem

Doctors spend significant time on documentation instead of patient care — notes, summaries and clinical records eat into the consultation itself.

Ownership

Sole founder — owned the full path from discovery to go-to-market:

  • Product discovery → architecture → development → testing → go-to-market → B2B partnerships.
  • Defined the product vision, technical architecture and feature roadmap.
  • Led B2B sales, demos and onboarding for early clinical users and partners, including the relationship with Hospital Clínica Bíblica, the largest private hospital in Costa Rica.

What I shipped

An AI clinical documentation platform that transforms consultations into structured medical documentation, using an OpenAI Whisper + GPT pipeline on a Python/Flask + PostgreSQL backend.

Impact

  • 20 physicians onboarded, 415 consultations run through the platform.
  • 173 commits, 1 contributor — built and shipped solo, from idea to production.
  • Designed to reduce repetitive documentation and let doctors complete records during or shortly after the consultation.

Clonocare — Principal Developer, AI Product
Asynchronous medical consultation platform
4-stage AI orchestration pipeline Real physician pilot
View case ↗

Problem

Patients needing a medical opinion often face long waits for a live appointment, and doctors need structured, reliable intake data before they can act on a case.

Ownership

Contracted as principal developer — owned the technical build end-to-end, from architecture to a completed physician pilot.

  • Designed and built the AI orchestration pipeline (classifier, conversational agent, and structuring layer) using the Claude API.
  • Built the doctor console and patient-facing flows in Bubble, including onboarding wizards and the payment verification flow.
  • Developed the backend on Xano: conversation history handling, lab results flow, doctor response endpoints with automated email, and a background job to close expired conversations.

What I shipped

  • 4-stage AI orchestration pipeline (classifier → conversational agent → structuring layer → payment verification) built on the Claude API.
  • 6 endpoints powering the doctor console alone, plus patient onboarding and payment verification flows.
  • Bubble frontend wired directly to the Xano backend through REST APIs.

Impact

  • Production-ready Claude API integration, validated through real physician consultations.
  • A physician completed a full pilot of the platform using it in real consultations.
  • Delivered under a signed software development contract, later expanded in scope.

Alisto — B2B Sales & Supplier Growth
Marketplace connecting restaurants & suppliers · Part-time
View case ↗

Part-time, alongside my full-time role at Tobipets.

Problem

Restaurants were ordering from suppliers through scattered WhatsApp threads, with no easy way to compare prices or track spending — and suppliers had no structured way to reach new restaurant clients.

Ownership

Worked in the sales and supplier-side growth area, focusing on:

  • Acquiring and onboarding new suppliers across key categories.
  • Building and executing the supplier acquisition strategy.
  • Maintaining long-term relationships and consistent communication with active suppliers.
  • Aligning expectations between restaurants and suppliers to improve fulfilment and product availability.

What I shipped

  • Centralised, structured ordering with multiple suppliers instead of scattered WhatsApp messages.
  • A cleaner product catalogue with comparable presentations across suppliers.
  • Clearer operational standards with suppliers for faster order confirmation.

Impact

  • Faster comparison of prices, presentations and availability for restaurant buyers.
  • New customer acquisition for suppliers through marketplace visibility.
  • Reduced friction by shifting suppliers from ad-hoc communication to platform-based order handling.
